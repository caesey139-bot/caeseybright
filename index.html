1. Who we are

This website is caeseybright.vercel.app, a personal blog run by Caesey Bright — a Grade 10 student in Nairobi, Kenya, documenting a journey toward studying Aerospace Engineering at MIT. This is a personal, non-commercial blog.

2. What information we collect

We only collect information you voluntarily provide:

  • Private messages — your name, email and message when you use the private question form.
  • YouTube notifications — your email if you sign up to be notified when the YouTube channel launches.
  • Comments — your name and comment when you leave a reply. Comments are stored locally in your browser only.

We do not collect any information automatically or use tracking of any kind.

3. How we use your information

Your information is used only to respond to your message personally or notify you when the YouTube channel launches. We will never sell, share, or distribute your data to any third party.

4. How your data is stored

Form submissions are stored securely in Supabase, a secure cloud database. You can read their privacy policy at supabase.com/privacy. Blog comments are stored only in your own browser and never sent to any server.

5. Cookies

This website does not use cookies. We do not track your browsing behaviour or use any analytics tools that collect personal data.

6. Google AdSense (future)

This site may in the future display Google AdSense advertisements. If this happens, Google may use cookies to serve relevant ads. You will be notified and this policy will be updated. Until then, no ads are shown and no ad-related data is collected.

7. Your rights

You have the right to request what information we hold about you, request deletion of your data, or withdraw consent at any time. To do so, use the private message form on this site.

8. Children's privacy

This blog is written by a minor and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before submitting any information.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Who I am

A high schooler with a dream
too big to ignore.

I'm Caesey Bright — a Grade 10 student at an ACE international school in Nairobi. Right now I'm working through geometry, supplementing with extra maths on Schoolhouse.world, and doing every club and activity I can. Trigonometry, pre-calculus, SAT prep — all of that is still ahead of me. But I'm building the foundation now so I'm ready when it comes.

I want to study Aerospace Engineering at MIT. I know I'm early in the journey — that's exactly why I'm documenting it. Not the polished version. The real one, from Grade 10 in Nairobi, where the dream is big and the road is long.

— Caesey
